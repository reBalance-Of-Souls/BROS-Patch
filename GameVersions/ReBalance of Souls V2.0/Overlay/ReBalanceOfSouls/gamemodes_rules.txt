# Extras > GAMEMODES RULES (replay.dll, 2026-11-22): what each Room Match game mode and option does.
# Same format as balance_changes.txt: an entry = its name alone on a line, ending in ":"; its lines under it, one tab
# a level; a line ending in ":" is a heading (yellow, shown without the ":"). Blank lines and # lines are skipped.
# "@gamemodes" alone on a line: every mode of gamemodes.txt goes there, in the Game Modes tab (the launcher writes
# gamemodes.txt from GameModes/<mode>/Mode.json: edit a mode's rules there, not here). These entries: the Room
# Options tab. ASCII only, and no '=' (the game's font has no such glyph: the line would fall back to Segoe UI).
# Wrapped and cut in pages by replay.dll (15 lines a page).
Game Mode:
	How it works:
		The room owner picks it on the Create room dialog's Game Mode row (Off: the normal rules). It changes the rules for every player in the room, on top of this update's balance.
	With the other rows:
		It works with Tag Battle, Team Battle, Multiplayer Team Battle, 2D Camera and Motion inputs. The room list shows the battle type, then the Game Mode and the options turned on.
	Same version:
		A player whose game does not have the same version of the mode cannot be Ready.

@gamemodes

Tag Battle:
	Two characters a side:
		One fights, the other waits on the bench: swap them during the fight.
	1v1, 2 players:
		Each player picks two characters: one in Battle Settings, the other with Character 2 in the lobby.
	1v2, 3 players:
		Team 1 is one player with two characters, team 2 two players with one character each. Join a team in the lobby: Ready waits until both teams are full.
	2v2, 4 players:
		Two players a team, one character each. The first to join a team starts the fight, the other one on the bench.
	Tag:
		Press two of Light, Heavy and Signature together, or your Rapid Reverse button.
	Tag in neutral:
		Free, from standing, walking, running, a step or a dash. The opponent freezes while your partner comes in, then your partner has a short recovery where it cannot act or guard: it can be punished.
	Tag in a combo:
		Costs 1.5 Reverse bars, once a combo, after the last hit of a move. The opponent stays in hitstun while your partner comes in. No Yellow Reverse for the rest of that combo.
	Kikon switch:
		Kikon, then hold Heavy: on hit, the opponent is forced to switch and its partner comes in with the blocked Kikon cinematic. No Konpaku is lost.
	KO:
		A character with no Konpaku left is out and its partner comes in. A side with no character left loses.
	Gauges:
		SP, Reverse, Fighting Spirit, HP and Konpaku are each character's own.
	Rapid Reverse:
		Only once your partner is out: until then, its input is the tag.

Team Battle:
	One after the other:
		KOF style: 2 or 3 characters a side, played in order. No tag.
	2v2 or 3v3, 2 players:
		Pick your characters in the lobby: Battle Settings, Character 2, then Character 3 in 3v3.
	KO:
		When a character loses its last Konpaku, the next one of its side comes in. A side with no character left loses.
	The one who made the KO:
		Keeps its HP and Konpaku (HP back up to 31% if it was under 30%). Back to base form, Fighting Spirit and SP at 0, Reverse full.
	Timer:
		The round timer starts again at each KO.
	The rest:
		Rapid Reverse works as in a normal match. No tag, no Kikon switch.

Multiplayer Team Battle:
	A player a character:
		Team Battle rules, but each character of a team is played by its own player.
	2v2, 4 players:
		Two players a team, one character each.
	1v2 (2v2), 3 players:
		Team 1 is one player with two characters, team 2 two players with one character each.
	Lobby:
		Join a team in the lobby: Ready waits until both teams are full.
	The rest:
		As in Team Battle: a KO brings the next character in, the one who made the KO keeps its HP and Konpaku with its gauges and form reset, and the timer starts again.

2D Camera:
	Side view:
		The fight is filmed from the side, as in a 2D fighting game. Your character starts on the left of your screen; the camera zooms out as the fighters move apart and turns with them when they sidestep around each other.
	Same game:
		Only the camera changes: moves, damage and combos are the normal ones. Cinematics (Kikon, Awakening, intros) keep their own cameras.
	Controls follow the screen:
		Left and right move you left and right on the screen: toward or away from your opponent depending on your side. Up and down sidestep into and out of the screen. Steps and Hoho take the same directions.
	For everyone:
		The room owner turns it on: every player in the room plays with it.

Motion inputs:
	Same game, new inputs:
		Moves, damage and combos are the normal ones: only the way you do them changes. Directions in numpad notation (6 is forward).
	Directions:
		With 2D Camera: read on the screen, 6 is toward your opponent. With the normal camera: read on the stick as you hold it (6 is right, 8 is up), whatever your side.
	Signature:
		236 + Light. Forward Signature: 623 + Light, back Signature: 421 + Light, for the characters that have one. Wherever a move waits for the Signature button (its next hit, a follow-up): 236 + Light.
	Specials:
		Reverse: 214 + Light. Hoho: 623 + Heavy. Tilt: 236 + Heavy (forward + Heavy is a plain Heavy). SP1: 236236 + Light. SP2: 236236 + Heavy.
	Kikon:
		214 + Heavy. To charge it, keep Heavy held, or do 214 + Heavy again and hold it, during its opening.
	Halibel and Yhwach:
		Halibel's side Signatures: 8 or 2 + Light with 2D Camera, 4 or 6 + Light with the normal camera. Yhwach's back SP1: 214214 + Light, his forward SP1: 41236 + Light.
	Buttons:
		The Signature and Kikon buttons and the SP trigger do nothing. Light, Heavy, Guard, Dash, Grab, Awakening (L3 + R3) and Rapid Reverse (Light + Heavy) stay. No motion dashes.
	For everyone:
		The room owner turns it on: every player in the room plays with it.
