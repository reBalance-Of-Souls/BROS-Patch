Universal Changes:
	Flashstep :
		vanish : 30 frames for every character, invincible the whole time (a few characters had 18 to 36)
		reappear : 24 frames of recovery for every character (was 20 to 30)
		whole flashstep : 54 frames for every character (was 40 to 66)
		a flashstep pressed in the last 3 frames of blockstun now comes out on the first free frame (it used to be lost)
	Flashstep follow-ups :
		damage -25% on every flashstep follow-up and on its up / down finishers
	Pushback on hit :
		the slide of a light hit reaction and the recoil after a Sig are now the same on every character (they used to depend on the size of the character who got hit)
		pushback on block is unchanged
	Diminishing returns :
		after each SP move in a combo, the attacker gains less Reverse gauge (Fighting Spirit is no longer reduced) for the rest of the combo : -25% after the 1st SP, -50% after the 2nd, -75% after the 3rd
		the hits of the SP itself still count at the previous rate
		the combo ends when the opponent leaves hitstun ; a Yellow Reverse resets it
	Losing a Konpaku :
		no Fighting Spirit for a Konpaku lost while Awakened or Reawakened
		in base form, the Fighting Spirit it gives is capped : the one who lost the Konpaku never ends up with a fuller bar than the one who took it
	Reverse :
		a Reverse done while the opponent is in hitstun is now the Yellow (Chain) Reverse instead of the White one ; Kikon > hold > Reverse stays White
		White Reverse Fighting Spirit gain : 0.0013 -> 0.00125 a frame (the original game's value)
	Low HP damage bonus (under 30% HP) : +20% -> +15%
	Grabs :
		a grab tech pressed up to 6 frames before the grab connects now techs it ; counter and punish grabs still cannot be teched
		online, the grab tech window is the same 12 frames for every player
		grab tech works while holding Step / Dash
		no SP1 / SP2 out of a held-grab run, for the rest of that run ; an SP out of a normal dash is unchanged
	Awakening and Reawakening :
		buttons pressed in the first 30 frames of your own Awakening / Reawakening are ignored (a grab pressed then used to come out and whiff right after it)
